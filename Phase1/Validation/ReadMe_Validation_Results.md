The results from the validation run at Re=200 were 0.194 for the Strouhal number and 0.64 for the maximum coefficient of lift. This is a 1.5% and 7.2% error respectively compared to the published data from Cox, J. S., Rumsey, C. L., Brentner, K. S., & Younis, B. A. , where the Strouhal number was determined to be around 0.197 and the maximum coefficient of lift was around 0.69. These results confirm that the meshing methodology produces physically valid results for a 2D laminar flow.
